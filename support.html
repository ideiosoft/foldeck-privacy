Where are my files saved?

Every result opens in a preview first. When you tap Save it is kept inside the app and listed in My Files, where you can open, share or delete it.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads

Open Remove Ads from the home screen and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are processed on your device. See the privacy policy.
